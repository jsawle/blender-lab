Glow and emissive strength are the new SDK 5 settings. The flames, coals and core colours come from the Blender models themselves.

What to look for

  • Do the lamp flames and brazier coals read at night from street level and from above?
  • Do emitters light nearby walls and floors, or only glow themselves?
  • Does the core glow cleanly through the cutaway without washing out the mantle?
  • Frame rate with glow on and off on a school Chromebook.

The house is a representative atrium house placed approximately in Regio VI, north-west of the Forum. It is not on a real plot.
